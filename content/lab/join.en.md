Students in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering and its graduate program can join the laboratory.

## Joining as a Master's Student

Please refer to the [university website](https://www.titech.ac.jp/prospective-students) for details.

### Internal Students and Students from Other Universities in Japan

Please take the graduate school entrance examination held in August. Anyone who satisfies the eligibility requirements set by the university may apply.

### International Students

Applications through IGP(C) are accepted twice a year. Applicants must satisfy the screening criteria of the department. IGP(A) is offered once a year. For IGP(A), applicants are expected to continue on to the doctoral program.

## Joining as a Doctoral Student or a Visiting Student

Please contact us individually.

## Research Students

As a general rule, we do not accept research students whose purpose is to prepare for the entrance examination for the master's program. If you wish to join as a research student for another purpose, please contact us individually.
