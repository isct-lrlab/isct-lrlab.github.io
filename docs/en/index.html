LRLab

Science Tokyo LRLab / Okumura-Funakoshi Group

LRLab aims to build intelligent systems that understand language and collaborate with people. Our research spans natural language processing, multimodal dialogue systems, knowledge processing, and the construction and evaluation of language resources, covering topics from fundamental research to applied research.
